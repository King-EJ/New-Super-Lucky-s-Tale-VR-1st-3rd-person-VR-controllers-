LUCKY VR  -  VR mod for "New Super Lucky's Tale" (Unity 2017.4, Mono x64, built-in renderer, BepInEx 5)
========================================================================================================
Version 0.1.3 (test build) (NOT TESTED FULL GAME)

INSTALL
  unzip to game folder, only tested with virtual desktop XR

  **[Nefarius ViGEmBus](https://github.com/nefarius/ViGEmBus/releases) support - with the driver installed (ViGEmBusSetup_x64.msi in the zip) the VR
        controllers are a real virtual Xbox 360 controller ([Controls] InputMethod = Auto / ViGEm / InputSystem).**

VIEW
  * Third person GameCamera (default): you stand where the game's camera is; when it turns / cuts, your view turns
    with it. The right stick moves the game's camera as usual.
  * Third person Follow: behind Lucky; the right stick turns you, the left stick walks where you look.
  * First person: through Lucky's eyes (his head hidden); the left stick walks where you look, the right stick turns.
  * **RIGHT STICK CLICK (tap) = first / third person**.   **HOLD R3 0.8 s = third person GameCamera / Follow**.
  * Pause / menus / loading: the game's picture on a screen in front of you. Cut-scenes: at the game's camera.
  * The HUD floats in front of you (the mod's own UI camera draws the game's HUD / menus into the VR screen).

ARMS
  * Lucky's arms follow your controllers (first person; third person too - [Arms] ArmsInThirdPerson).
  * LIVE HAND ADJUST: hold BOTH GRIPS 1 s (or VR menu > Arms). Right stick = tilt / turn, right trigger + right stick
    = roll, left stick = move left / right / forward / back, left trigger + left stick = up / down, X = other hand,
    B = reset, A = done (saved).



CONTROLS (the VR controllers are an Xbox 360 controller for the game)

  (VR settings > Controls > "Swap X and B" swaps the left X and right B buttons)
  
  Left stick click .. START (tap) / BACK (hold 0.6 s)
  
  **Right stick click . tap = first / third person, hold = GameCamera / Follow (menus: R3)**
  
  Both grips 1 s .... live hand adjust
  
  Both stick clicks . re-centre (hold 1.2 s = VR settings menu)

Disable: to disable Mod rename winhttp.dll to winhttp.dll.bak
